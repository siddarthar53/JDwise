# JD Style Guide

## Voice and tone
Address the candidate directly as "you". Use plain, specific language. Prefer active verbs ("Lead", "Build", "Own") at the start of each responsibility bullet. Avoid internal jargon and acronyms without explanation.

## Structure
Every job description has: a 2-4 sentence summary, 4-7 responsibility bullets, 3-6 qualification bullets, a skills list of 3-10 items, employment type, location or work mode, and a salary range where pay transparency applies.

## Requirements
List only requirements that are truly needed on day one. Separate must-haves from nice-to-haves by appending "(nice to have)". Keep years-of-experience consistent with the level: junior 0-2, mid 2-5, senior 5+, staff 8+, principal 10+.

## Inclusive language
Use gender-neutral language. Do not reference age, national origin, or physical traits unless essential to the role. Replace jargon such as "rockstar" or "ninja" with concrete descriptions of the skill required.

## Compensation
Include the base salary range for roles in locations with pay-transparency laws and keep it within the approved band for the role family, level and region.
